I'm a rare executive who stepped away from organizational leadership to build with AI from first principles — and that hands-on technical depth is what now shapes how I lead. It started at Huawei's Central Research Institute, shaping the architecture for next-gen, context-aware AI systems, and it became personal when I co-founded a startup building a human-powered, AI platform at the intersection of health, data, and AI — a care support platform for families with special needs.

Today, as Chief Data and AI Officer, I lead global data and AI strategy — building trusted, intelligent systems and championing a culture of data literacy and ethical AI adoption. I still stay in the build, not just above it: using AI tools directly for product design, rapid prototyping, and QA, the same way I did as a co-founder.

How I Build

I think in products, and it always starts with a problem, not a piece of technology. I dig into a real pain point, ask the questions nobody wants to sit through, and only then start building. These days that means turning an idea into a working prototype fast, using AI and synthetic data to show real value and get honest feedback, instead of a slide deck.

From there it's iteration: testing with customers, working closely with design, refining based on what actually lands versus what I assumed would. Once it's proven, I hand it off to engineering to make it fast, resilient, and built to scale.

AI has genuinely stretched what I can do myself. I used to stop at defining requirements and scoping the product.
