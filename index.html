Marketplace

Customer data platform without vendor lock-in

Customer data was fragmented across analytics platforms and vendor warehouses, so teams lacked a real-time single…

Challenge
Customer data was fragmented across analytics platforms and vendor warehouses, so teams lacked a real-time single view of customer signals while vendor costs scaled rapidly.
Built
Architected and deployed a modular CDP on open source to replace proprietary vendor systems. Decoupled ingestion, real-time stream processing, open-source storage, and governance APIs so integrations could evolve without vendor lock-in.
Impact
Migrated multi-petabyte analytics onto an open-source stack and lowered TCO. Established a trusted real-time source of truth for profiles and behavioral signals that powers personalized buyer and seller experiences. Teams can plug in pipelines, AI models, and apps without disrupting core operations.

Telecom

Real-time network health & churn prevention on Spark

Telcos sat on petabytes of network telemetry and subscriber data but could not analyze either fast enough to…

Challenge
Telcos sat on petabytes of network telemetry and subscriber data but could not analyze either fast enough to catch outages or save at-risk customers.
Built
Architected an enterprise Spark platform: Structured Streaming scored 4G/5G telemetry for anomalies in near real-time, while MLlib generated live churn-risk scores from usage and service data.
Impact
Anomalies now trigger self-healing routines or maintenance alerts before regional outages, and high-value subscribers get instant retention offers the moment risk spikes.
