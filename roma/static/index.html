Roma

A flight-search agent. Tell it a route and a date and it will price the trip, say whether today looks worth taking, and show you the rule it used to decide.

Its fares are simulated by its own model, not pulled from a live market. For real prices it hands you off to Google Flights, Kayak, Expedia and Priceline.
